The 12-Week AI Sprint: From Idea to Production

DRAFT. Pending review by Innovsol before publication.

Long, open-ended consulting engagements are a common reason AI initiatives lose momentum. Our delivery model compresses the journey into phases with clear outputs, so you can see progress and decide at each stage whether to continue.

Discover (weeks 1–2)

AI maturity assessment, stakeholder interviews, a review of the data landscape and prioritisation of use cases. Output: an AI opportunity map and a prioritised use-case backlog.

Design (weeks 3–4)

Technical architecture, technology selection, a security and governance framework, team structure and a sprint-by-sprint build plan. Output: a technical blueprint and a security and governance framework.

Deploy (weeks 5–12)

Iterative build with embedded engineers, release to real users, and hardening for production: monitoring, evaluation and operational runbooks.

Scale (week 13 onward)

Extend to further use cases, teams or regions, and keep improving models and outcomes against the metrics agreed at the start.

Why it works

Each phase ends with a deliverable, a decision and a fixed next step. You are never committed to more than the next phase. The details of how we engage are on the engagement models page.
