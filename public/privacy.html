Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Innovsol ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Contact form: name, work email, phone (optional), company, industry, enquiry type and your message.
  • Email links: when you use "Apply Now" or "Download Playbook", your own email app sends us the details you choose to include, including any resume you attach.
  • Technical data: basic server logs (IP address, browser, pages requested) used for security and reliability.

How we use it

To respond to your enquiry, evaluate job applications, send requested materials, and keep the site secure. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run the site (for example email delivery and hosting) and where required by law. This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and some images from Unsplash, which may receive your IP address.

Retention

Enquiries are kept for as long as needed to respond and for reasonable business records. Job applications are kept for up to 12 months unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

Your rights

You can request access, correction or deletion of your personal information by emailing hello@innovsol.ai.

Candidate portal and job applications

If you create a portal account or apply for a role we process your name, email, password (stored hashed by our authentication provider), cover note, resume and application status. Resumes are kept in private storage visible only to you and Innovsol hiring staff. Applications are kept for up to 12 months after the role closes unless you ask us to delete them sooner. You can delete your account and all associated data yourself from the portal.

Service providers (processors)

We use: a hosting provider for this website; Supabase for the candidate portal database, authentication and file storage; an email delivery provider for messages we send; and, only if you opt in, an analytics provider. They process data on our instructions.

Consent and legal basis

We process enquiry and application data to respond to your request and, for applications, to take steps at your request before a possible employment relationship. Optional analytics run only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time from "Cookie settings" in the footer. Where India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 or the EU/UK GDPR applies, you have the rights described below.

International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as contractual clauses.

Children

This website is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Security

We use encryption in transit, input validation, row-level access controls and optional multi-factor authentication to protect information. No method of transmission is completely secure.

Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@innovsol.ai.
