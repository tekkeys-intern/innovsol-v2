Cookie Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This website is designed to work without tracking cookies.

What we store

  • Strictly necessary: none required to browse the site.
  • Preferences: if you click "Save Job" on a job page, your choice is stored in your browser's local storage on your own device. It is never sent to us.
  • Consent choice: if analytics is enabled on the site, your accept / decline choice is stored in your browser's local storage so we do not ask again on every page.
  • Analytics (optional): only when enabled by us and accepted by you. It may set cookies to count visits and see which pages are useful. No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies are used. Change your mind any time via "Cookie settings" in the footer.

Third-party content

Fonts (Google Fonts) and some images (Unsplash) are loaded from those providers and may set their own technical data. See their policies for details.

Managing storage

You can clear local storage and cookies at any time from your browser settings.
